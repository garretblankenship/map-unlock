# Open World Life — Privacy Policy

_Draft for review. Last updated: [date of publication]._

Open World Life is a game that clears a fog from the map as you explore the
real world. It is built so that your location and your progress stay on your
phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your location.** The app uses your phone's location to work out which
  parts of the map you've visited. Location is processed on your phone only.
  It is never sent to us, and we do not keep any record of where you have
  been.
- **Your progress.** The areas you've unlocked, the regions you've visited
  and your settings are stored on your phone. There are no accounts and no
  servers holding your data.

If your phone's own backup is turned on (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on
Android), your progress is included in that backup so it can be restored on
a new phone. That backup is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy
terms; we cannot access it.

## What the app downloads

To show the map and the regions you explore, the app downloads public data
from these services. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address
and which part of the map is requested, which is needed to send the right
data. They do not receive your location history, and we do not send them
any information about you.

- **OpenFreeMap** (tiles.openfreemap.org): map images and map data,
  including lakes and rivers. https://openfreemap.org
- **[Your boundary host]** ([BOUNDARIES_URL]): region boundaries
  (neighbourhoods, cities, parks and so on).
- **[Your map style host]** ([MAP_STYLE_URL], jsDelivr by default): the map's
  style, when you save an area for offline use.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or data brokers.
- No sale or sharing of personal data.

## Sharing

If you choose to share your progress, the app makes an image containing a
region's outline, its name and your percentage, and passes it to your
phone's share sheet. It never includes the streets you've explored. Where it
goes after that is up to you.

## Permissions

- **Location (while using the app):** to clear the fog where you are.
- **Location (always), optional:** to keep clearing the fog while the phone
  is in your pocket. You can turn this off in the app or in your phone's
  settings at any time.

## Deleting your data

All data is on your phone. Use **Settings → Reset all progress** in the app,
or delete the app, to remove it.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.
