## Description

Explore the real world like an open-world game.

The map starts covered in fog. Everywhere you go — walking, hiking, cycling,
driving or on the train — the fog clears. Every neighbourhood, city, county,
park, state and country you visit gets its own progress score, so you can
set out to unlock 100% of your town, your favourite national park, or a
whole country.

• Fog of war for the real world: see exactly where you've been, and where
  you haven't.
• Progress for every place: neighbourhoods, cities, counties, parks and
  forests, states and countries. Water doesn't count against you.
• Built for the outdoors: works with no signal. Save areas before a trip and
  the map, lakes and rivers, and park boundaries come with you.
• Keeps exploring in your pocket, and eases off the GPS when you stop to
  save battery.
• Share your progress: a card with the region's outline and your percentage,
  never the streets you've explored.
• Private by design: your location never leaves your phone. No accounts, no
  ads, no tracking.
• Dark mode, miles or kilometres.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, map tiles by OpenFreeMap.
